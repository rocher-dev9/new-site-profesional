What I do.

Five directions where I combine visual design with strategic thinking. They aren't silos: they overlap, feed each other and, in the end, speak about the same project from different angles.

01

Visual design & art direction

From concept to final piece. I shape your brand's visual language — typography, color, composition and graphic system — so every touchpoint speaks the same language.

02

Branding & corporate identity

I build identities that scale: from the symbol to complete brand guidelines. Pieces that work on paper, on screen, at a trade fair stand and in an Instagram story.

03

UI/UX & interface design

Wireframes, prototypes and final design in Figma, focused on hierarchy, accessibility and clear flows. IoT platforms, industrial dashboards and B2B portals where usability isn't optional.

04

Digital marketing & content

Strategy and execution for social media and advertising campaigns. Content calendars, post packs and metrics analysis, thinking about the feed and not just the individual piece.

  • Content calendar
  • Post packs
  • Campaigns
  • Metrics
05

AI applied to design

I use Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini as tools to speed up the process, not to replace judgment. Iterations that used to take weeks are now solved in days.

  • Claude
  • ChatGPT
  • Gemini
  • Human judgment
