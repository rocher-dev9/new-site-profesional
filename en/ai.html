/ process

Four phases. One direction.

AI doesn't replace any phase of design: it speeds up the most time-consuming ones so the effort goes where it truly makes a difference.

  1. 01

    Explore

    Market research, references and creative territories. AI widens the range; I choose what's worth developing.

  2. 02

    Generate

    Concept variants, copy, palettes and content structures in little time, to compare real options rather than hypotheses.

  3. 03

    Refine

    This is where craft takes over: typography, composition, brand tone and detail. AI assists; the final design is mine.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Analysis of metrics and campaign results to understand what works and feed the next iteration.

/ AI agents

Agents that think in steps.

An agent doesn't just answer: it receives a goal, breaks it into steps, uses tools and carries out several tasks in a row. I design them to work within clear limits and always under supervision.

agent · market research
  1. Goal Analyze 5 competitors of a specialty coffee brand
  2. Plan search → comparison → synthesis
  3. Tool web search · 5 brands identified
  4. Tool social media analysis · tone, formats and frequency
  5. Synthesis 3 differentiation opportunities found
  6. Review report ready for approval
